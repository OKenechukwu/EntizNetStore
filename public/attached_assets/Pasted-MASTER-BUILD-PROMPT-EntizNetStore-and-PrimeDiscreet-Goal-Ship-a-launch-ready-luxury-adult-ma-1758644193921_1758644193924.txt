Goal: Ship a launch-ready, luxury, adult marketplace (physical + digital) with dual brand fronts (EntizNetStore & PrimeDiscreet) on one backend. Include Google AdSense, seller-run ads, feature banners, recommendations, short product videos, uncensored media (within law), advanced buyer profiles, enterprise-grade seller dashboard, address reconfirmation, messaging, regional payments, dark/light modes, award-worthy UX in gold & black aligned with EntizNet.

0) Tech Stack & Principles

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase (Auth, DB, Storage, RLS).

PWA (installable, offline shells for catalog, add-to-home-screen).

CDN & asset pipelines: Next Image for images, HLS/mp4 for videos via storage + signed URLs; lazy-load + skeletons.

Search: Typesense/Algolia ready (start with Supabase full-text; abstraction layer to swap).

i18n & multi-currency (base USD; FX util; display in user locale).

Performance: route-level caching, ISR where safe, client hints, prefetch on hover, code-split.

Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA (focus rings, landmarks, ARIA for carousels/video controls).

7) Payments & Escrow (Regional)

Gateways (abstracted adapter; select by user geo & seller region):

Asia: GCash, Maya/PayMaya, Alipay.

Americas: Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay.

Europe: Stripe, PayPal, Klarna, SEPA.

Africa: Flutterwave, Paystack, M-Pesa.

Escrow logic:

Funds captured → held by platform.

Physical: release on buyer “Delivered & OK” or auto-release after X days post-carrier delivered if no dispute.

Digital: release on first successful download/view confirmation + short dispute window.

Payouts to sellers: bank or e-wallet; KYC required; rolling reserve configurable.
